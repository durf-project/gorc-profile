## About this profile

A Global Open Research Commons (GORC) profile describes a commons with the [GORC International Model](https://doi.org/10.15497/RDA00119) of the Research Data Alliance: who takes care of a shared resource, under which rules, with which services, and what it takes to keep it going. This profile does that for the **Dutch Repository Federation Commons**: the libraries and partners that together take care of the **Dutch Scholarly Communication Corpus**.

The profile has two scenarios. Switch between them at the top of the page.

- **IST** is the situation as it is now, in November 2026.
- **SOLL** is where we want to be at the end of the DURF project, in May 2030. It is explicitly a **draft**: an invitation to the community to shape it with us.

The honeycomb above shows the ten essential elements of the GORC model. Their colour shows how far each element is in place, based on a self-assessment with the [SPII Principles Alignment Tool](https://surf-ori.github.io/spii-1b-principles-alignment-tool/#gorc). Point at an element for a short explanation, click it to read the section.

This profile is the basis of the **Federation Rule Book**, a deliverable of DURF theme 1 (Governance): the governance structure and the system of agreements (*afsprakenstelsel*) of the federation. See [From profile to rule book](#rule-book).
