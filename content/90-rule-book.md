## From profile to rule book

The **Federation Rule Book** is a deliverable of DURF theme 1: the governance structure and system of agreements (*afsprakenstelsel*) of the Dutch Repository Federation. This profile is its basis: every chapter of the rule book answers what one or more GORC elements ask for. The SOLL scenario is the first draft of its content.

| Chapter of the rule book | Built on | Ostrom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Purpose, mission and the corpus | [Research objects](#research-objects), [Governance](#governance) | 1 |
| 2. Members and roles in the information chain | [Rules of participation](#rules), [Services and tools](#services) | 1, 2 |
| 3. Governance structure and decision making | [Governance](#governance) | 3, 7, 8 |
| 4. Standards and data contracts | [Standards](#standards), [Interoperability](#interoperability) | 2 |
| 5. Monitoring and quality targets | [Interoperability](#interoperability), [Governance](#governance) | 4 |
| 6. Accountability, solidarity and conflict resolution | [Rules of participation](#rules) | 5, 6 |
| 7. Funding and continuity | [Sustainability](#sustainability) | |
| 8. People, skills and support | [Human capacity](#human-capacity) | |
| 9. Engagement and change of the rule book | [Engagement](#engagement) | 3 |

:::ist
The rule book does not exist yet. The building blocks are the [position paper](https://durf-project.github.io/position-paper/) (vision and commitments), its roles and responsibilities matrix, the [data contracts](https://durf-project.github.io/durf-data-mesh-architecture/) and the [dashboards](https://durf-project.github.io/dashboards/).
:::

:::soll
The rule book is adopted by the general assembly, signed by the members, published openly and changed only through the general assembly, with a version history. It is reviewed towards the end of the DURF project so that it keeps working after May 2030.
:::
