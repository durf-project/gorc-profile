Services and tools are what people use in the commons. For the federation they are the components of the **DURF information chain** that carry the corpus: the repositories and CRIS systems, the OpenAIRE services PROVIDE, Graph, Broker and Connect, the KB e-Depot and URN:NBN resolver, and, outside the federation, the publishers (with Crossref) and the discovery channels. Defining them draws the boundary of the commons (Ostrom 1). Their roles and responsibilities are set out in the [position paper](https://durf-project.github.io/position-paper/#roles-responsibilities-and-parties).

![The DURF information chain, with the six DURF themes marked as puzzle pieces](https://durf-project.github.io/position-paper/assets/durf-information-chain.svg)

:::ist
### Now: the chain exists, but not as one system

- About 20 institutional **repositories and CRIS systems** hold the corpus; two thirds of their records have no file.
- **OpenAIRE PROVIDE** harvests and validates, the **Graph** deduplicates and links, the **Broker** could return enrichments, but the feedback loop is not used yet.
- The **Netherlands Research Portal** (OpenAIRE Connect, [netherlands.openaire.eu](https://netherlands.openaire.eu)) shows the Dutch slice.
- The **KB e-Depot** preserves what institutions deliver, and the **URN:NBN resolver** keeps it citable.
- The [DURF dashboards](https://durf-project.github.io/dashboards/) monitor metadata and full texts as a proof of concept.

Each service is maintained by its own party, and there is no shared view on their continuity, or a catalogue of them.
:::

:::soll
### Target: every service has an owner and a contract

Every service in the chain has an **owner**, a **data contract** for what goes in and out, and a **continuity arrangement**, all listed in one catalogue. The full loop runs: harvest and validate, deduplicate, enrich and feed back to the source, preserve and resolve, and show in the portal. The portal is managed together by the members. The dashboards become a maintained service of the federation. There is one contact point for questions.
:::
