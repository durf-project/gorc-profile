In GORC, governance is about the purpose of the commons and the strategy, values and policies that frame how that purpose is pursued: mission and vision, strategic plans, risk management, and the structures through which decisions are made. For a federated commons like ours, it is about how independent institutions decide together without creating a new organisation.

:::ist
### Now: a project, not yet a federation

Today the federation is organised by the **DURF project**: a project lead, six themes with theme leads, a tech lead, supporting roles and a steering group. Around it are existing bodies where decisions about parts of the chain are made: **UKB** (library directors), **WISH** (repository managers), **PUGNL** (CRIS managers), **NaMeCo** (Dutch members of OpenAIRE) and the **Chiefs Open Science of UNL**.

What is missing is a governance of the corpus itself:

- the purpose and mission are written in the [position paper](https://durf-project.github.io/position-paper/), but it is not yet signed;
- it is not written down who decides what for the federation, outside the project;
- there is no risk or continuity plan, although resilience is the reason the federation exists;
- there is no agreed way to deal with a party that does not deliver what was agreed, or with disagreements.

The mandate comes from outside: the UNL Open Science Agenda asks for a federation of repositories, and Open Science NL funds the project until May 2030.
:::

:::soll
### Target: shared governance inside what already exists

We do not want to create a new organisation. The proposal is that the federation starts **within UKB**, and is **open to non-UKB members**: any Dutch research performing organisation can join at its own pace (Ostrom 7 and 8).

| Body | Role in the federation |
| --- | --- |
| General assembly: the annual **DURF Federation Day** | All parties meet, report on their commitments, adopt changes to the rule book (Ostrom 3) |
| **Coordinating board** (small, from UKB, KB and OpenAIRE, with seats for non-UKB members) | Takes decisions between assemblies, monitors the dashboards, handles signals and conflicts (Ostrom 5, 6) |
| **NL Research Information Content Board** (formerly WISH, through EduStandaard) | Stewards the metadata standard |
| **NaMeCo** | Formal representation of Dutch institutions in OpenAIRE |
| Theme working groups | Prepare agreements per theme: metadata, full text, preservation, discovery, portal |

The board speaks for the federation towards OpenAIRE, publishers, UNL and the ministry. Decisions, the rule book and its changes are public. A risk and continuity plan describes what happens when a repository, OpenAIRE or the e-Depot drops out, and the restore procedure is tested.

**Help shape this:** is UKB the right home? Who sits on the coordinating board? How do non-UKB members get a voice?
:::
