Rules of participation and access set the rights, obligations and accountability of everyone in the commons: who may join, what each party contributes and can expect in return, how resources may be used, and what happens when rules are broken. GORC follows Ostrom: the rules should fit local values and practices.

![The DURF information chain: repositories and CRIS systems feed OpenAIRE PROVIDE and the OpenAIRE Graph; from there metadata is enriched, made discoverable through the Netherlands Research Portal and preserved in the KB e-Depot](https://durf-project.github.io/position-paper/assets/durf-information-chain.svg)

:::ist
### Now: everyone assumes it works

There are no written rules of participation. Everyone assumes the chain works and that they can count on each other, but nothing is signed on:

- who can join the federation, and how;
- which role each party plays in the information chain, and what it can expect in return;
- which standards and quality levels apply;
- solidarity when a party cannot deliver, and how conflicts are resolved.

The position paper and its [roles and responsibilities matrix](https://durf-project.github.io/position-paper/#b-roles-and-responsibilities-matrix) are the first draft.
:::

:::soll
### Target: roles with reciprocity

Every box in the information chain is a **role**, every role is taken by a **party**, and each party takes on a responsibility and can expect something in return (from the position paper):

| Role | Responsibility | Party |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Institutional repository / CRIS | Capture and hold the institution's output and metadata, deliver to the Graph and the e-Depot | Each university library and other member |
| Harvester and validator (OpenAIRE PROVIDE) | Collect and check the records | OpenAIRE |
| OpenAIRE Graph and Broker | Aggregate, deduplicate, enrich and feed back | OpenAIRE |
| Netherlands Research Portal | Curated national outlet | OpenAIRE, with editorial management by the members |
| e-Depot and URN:NBN resolver | Preserve and keep citable | KB |

**Joining:** the core parties are the 13 university libraries, the KB and OpenAIRE. Every other Dutch research performing organisation can join at its own pace, starting with one role (for example: deliver to the Graph) and growing from there.

**Access:** the corpus metadata is open under CC0; full texts follow their own licences ("as open as possible, as closed as necessary").

**Accountability, in steps** (Ostrom 5 and 6): the dashboards show where agreements are not met; the first response is a signal and help from peers; then a conversation with the coordinating board; only if a problem persists, firmer steps decided by the general assembly. Helping comes before punishing.

**Help shape this:** what is the minimum a new member must deliver? Which steps are acceptable when a party does not deliver?
:::
