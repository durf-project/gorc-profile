Engagement is the ongoing interaction between the commons and its community: gathering needs, consulting, communicating, training and partnering. GORC warns that a commons that loses the trust of its community loses its mandate. For DURF, engagement is about showing the value of the corpus so that people want to take care of it.

![The DURF Federation Day 2026 at SURF in Utrecht](https://durf-project.github.io/project-page/assets/img/federation-day-2026.jpg)

:::ist
### Now: a project that communicates

DURF has a [project page](https://durf-project.github.io/project-page/), a position paper open for comments, a feedback button on every page, and held the first **DURF Federation Day** on 18 November 2026. Engagement is still project communication: there is no plan for the federation as a commons, and the value of the corpus is not yet visible to its users.
:::

:::soll
### Target: the corpus in use

The themes engage with the people who use the corpus, so that its value shows:

1. **Policy makers** at institutions, UNL and the government use the corpus, through the OpenAIRE Graph, for reporting.
2. **The public and journalists** use the corpus through the portal to find Dutch research, experts and projects, for example through the KB's [WijsNL](https://www.kb.nl/over-ons/projecten/wijsnl).
3. **Institutions** see their own progress on the dashboards, and good practice is recognised at the Federation Day.

The rule book and this profile are written in the open. Every piece of feedback gets an answer: accepted, parked or rejected, with a reason.

**Help shape this:** who else should use the corpus? What would make your institution want to take part?
:::
