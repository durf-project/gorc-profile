Human capacity is about the people: those who run the commons, those who contribute to it and those who use it, and the skills, training and working conditions they need. GORC asks for succession plans and documented processes so the commons does not depend on a few people.

:::ist
### Now: known people, spread out

To connect the systems we need to know the people at the partner institutions who work on them, and the existing user, expert and working groups: repository managers (WISH), CRIS managers (PUGNL), metadata experts, the UKB working groups such as licensing. They are known within their institutions and groups, but there is no overview across the federation, and much of the knowledge sits with a few people. Smaller institutions often lack the capacity to implement new standards.
:::

:::soll
### Target: a network of roles

- A **public register of roles**: for each member, who is the contact for the repository, the CRIS, metadata, full text capture and the e-Depot, with a backup.
- The skills each role needs are described in the rule book.
- **Peer support and train-the-trainer** sessions per theme, shared at the Federation Day and online, so larger institutions help smaller ones.

**Help shape this:** which roles does your institution have, and which are missing?
:::
